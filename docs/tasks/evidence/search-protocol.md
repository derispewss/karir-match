# Search protocol – Phase 1

## Databases
- Google Scholar
- IEEE Xplore
- ACM Digital Library
- Scopus (if available)

## Search strings
- "job retrieval" OR "job recommendation" AND (TF-IDF OR "cosine similarity")
- (add additional strings as needed)

## Inclusion criteria
- Publication year: 2021‑2026
- Language: English or Bahasa Indonesia
- Full‑text accessible
- Direct relevance to job retrieval/recommendation

## Exclusion criteria
- Opinion pieces without data
- Domains unrelated to employment search
- Duplicate records
